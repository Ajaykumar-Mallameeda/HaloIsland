# Support

Halo is an early-stage, source-only project maintained in spare time. Support is best effort.

## Getting help

- **Build or setup problems:** read the [README](README.md) first, especially
  [Requirements](README.md#requirements) and [Build and run](README.md#build-and-run).
- **Bugs:** open a [bug report](https://github.com/Ajaykumar-Mallameeda/HaloIsland/issues/new/choose). Include your
  Windows version, display scaling, the Halo commit, and the steps to reproduce the problem.
- **Feature ideas:** open a
  [feature request](https://github.com/Ajaykumar-Mallameeda/HaloIsland/issues/new/choose).
- **Security vulnerabilities:** do **not** open an issue. Follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

## Collecting diagnostics

Halo writes a local diagnostic log to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ProjectHalo\logs\halo.log`. It is designed to contain only
event names, counts and fixed tokens, never your content. Still, read it before you attach it to an issue, and remove
anything you do not want to share.

## What is not supported

- Binary installers and signed releases do not exist yet. Please do not ask for a download link; build from source.
- Multi-monitor setups, mixed DPI and Windows 10 are untested. Reports are welcome, but fixes may take time.
